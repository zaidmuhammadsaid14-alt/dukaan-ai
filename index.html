DukaanLink MultiVendor Ecommerce Platform

Build a modern, original, and fully responsive multi-vendor e-commerce web application named DukaanLink. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE: Clean tech-startup aesthetic only (Deep Blue #2563eb, Slate gray backgrounds, minimalist UI). Absolutely NO e-commerce giant clones, no fake pharmacy tags, and no template copies. It must look completely unique and professional. Core Features Required:

  1. User Authentication & Role Selection: Secure login with a role-selection screen right after signup where users can choose between 'Customer Shopping Mode' and 'Seller Store Dashboard'.
  2. Seller Dashboard & Inventory: Sellers can manage their store inventory, view products, and click an '+ Add Item' button to publish new products (Title, Price, Stock) instantly to the live feed.
  3. Customer Marketplace Feed: A clean home screen featuring a professional top header, search bar, modern welcome banner, and a grid of products fetched dynamically from the database.
  4. Interactive Shopping Bag / Cart: Customers can add products to their cart, view selected items, and manage total pricing seamlessly.
  5. Real-Time Customer-to-Seller Chat (Messaging): A functional messaging system where customers can send direct inquiries to store owners regarding products, and sellers can reply, maintaining a clean chat history list.
  6. Bottom Navigation Bar: Mobile-first bottom navigation with smooth switching between Home, Chats, Cart, and Profile screens.

DukaanLink is built and fully working. Here's what your app does:

Getting started: New users sign up with their name, email, and password, then land on a role-selection screen where they choose between Customer Shopping Mode (browse and buy) or Seller Store Dashboard (manage inventory). They can switch roles anytime from their profile.

Customer experience: A clean marketplace home screen with a branded header, live search bar, a welcome banner showing live product and store counts, category filter chips, and a responsive 2-column product grid. Tapping a product opens a detail sheet with quantity controls, add-to-cart, and a direct "message seller" button. The cart screen shows all bag items with quantity steppers, remove buttons, and a full price breakdown (subtotal, shipping, total).
